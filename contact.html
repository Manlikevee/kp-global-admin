Common questions

What time should I arrive?

Doors open 30 minutes before every service. Arriving a little early gives you time to find a seat and meet people.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available at the Main Campus, and our ushers will help you find a space.

Can I watch online instead?

Of course. Every Sunday service is streamed live and the sermons are posted on our sermons page afterwards.

How can I speak to a pastor?

Send us a message using the form and tell us what you would like to talk about. A member of the pastoral team will reply.
