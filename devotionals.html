Walking in Faith

Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.
Hebrews 11:1 (KJV)

Faith is not the absence of doubt. It is choosing to take the next step while the way ahead is still unclear.

Abraham left home without a map. Noah built before it rained. Neither of them had the full picture, but both trusted the One who did.

Today, name one thing you are waiting on God for, and thank Him in advance for His answer.

Today’s prayer

Lord, strengthen my trust in You when I cannot see the way. Help me take the next step today. Amen.
