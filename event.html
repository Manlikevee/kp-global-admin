Annual Thanksgiving Service

A special service of thanks for everything God has done this year. Invite a friend and stay for lunch.

Once a year we set aside a Sunday to simply say thank you. Expect heartfelt worship, testimonies from church members, a short message from Pastor Ade and a shared meal afterwards.

What to expect

  • Worship led by the choir
  • Testimonies and praise reports
  • Children’s church runs at the same time
  • Lunch together after the service
Date
Sunday 8 November 2026
Time
10:00 AM to 1:00 PM
Where
