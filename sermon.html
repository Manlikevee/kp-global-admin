Grace That Sustains

God’s grace is not only for the beginning of faith. It carries us through every season.

Video42:10

In this message from 2 Corinthians 12:9, Pastor Ade looks at Paul’s thorn in the flesh and the answer he received: “My grace is sufficient for thee.” Grace is not a last resort. It is the strength we live from every day.

Key points

  • Grace meets us in weakness, not only in strength.
  • God’s answer is sometimes presence, not removal.
  • Dependence on Him is where real strength begins.
My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness.

2 Corinthians 12:9 (KJV)
